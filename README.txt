Maps your Zigbee, Thread & Matter and Z-Wave networks: every device, the route it takes back to Homey, and a quality grade on every link, with the weakest links listed first. Most devices reach Homey through each other, so one bad relay can slow down a whole branch; the map shows you which one. Z-Wave is drawn straight from Homey, since Homey doesn't let apps read its routes.

The map is in the app's settings and in a dashboard widget. Switch on Browser view in the settings for the full visualizer on your local network, with a snapshot history that shows what changed over time. It has no login, so anyone on your network can open it while it is on.
